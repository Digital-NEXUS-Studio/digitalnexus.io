RigSafe: Sling & Crane Load Calculator — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026
Developer: Digital NEXUS (digitalnexus.studio)
Package: com.rigsafe.nexus

1. Summary

RigSafe stores everything you enter only on your phone. Digital NEXUS does not collect, receive, sell, or share any of your data. The app has no account, no ads, no analytics, and no in-app purchases.

2. What the app stores

The app keeps your local lift plan inputs, sling geometry selections, asymmetric center-of-gravity parameters, outrigger bearing notes, and saved lift worksheets in private device local storage. It is never uploaded or sent anywhere by the app.

3. Permissions

The app requests zero runtime device permissions (no camera, microphone, location, contacts, or file-system access). The Android manifest declares zero network (INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) permissions and the app executes zero outbound network requests.

4. Internet use

RigSafe runs completely offline. All assets, ASME B30.9 reference tables, and rigging math modules are bundled locally on your phone with zero external fonts or network calls.

5. Payments

RigSafe is completely free and includes no Google Play Billing library, subscriptions, or paywalls.

6. Sharing and printing

If you tap Print Worksheet or Share Text Summary, the generated lift worksheet is sent only to the Android print service or messaging app you choose.

7. Deleting your data

You can clear saved lift plans inside the app, clear app storage in Android settings, or uninstall RigSafe to remove all local data permanently.

8. Independence

RigSafe is an independent rigging estimation tool by Digital NEXUS. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or a substitute for ASME B30.9/B30.26 standards, manufacturer load charts, or Qualified Rigger / Lift Director sign-off.
