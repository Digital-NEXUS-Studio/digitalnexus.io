ToolVault: Jobsite Tool Log: Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026
Developer: Digital NEXUS (digitalnexus.studio)
Package: com.toolvault.nexus

1. Summary

ToolVault keeps your tool log on your phone. Digital NEXUS does not collect, receive, sell or share any of your data. The app has no account, no ads and no analytics. There is nothing to buy.

2. What the app stores

Your tools (name, brand, model, serial number, asset tag, value, status, location, the person who has it and the next service date), check-out and check-in records (person, place, time, notes and the signature drawn on the screen) and service records (date, what was done, cost, who did it and notes). Tag numbers are typed in; the app does not scan barcodes with the camera. This data is saved in the app's private storage on your device. The app never sends it to us or to anyone else.

3. Permissions

From version 1.1.3 the app asks for no permissions: no camera, no location, no microphone, no contacts and no files. It only declares the standard internet and network-state permissions, which need no approval, and it makes no network requests: everything works offline. The app contains no billing library and nothing can be bought in it. (Earlier versions asked for camera and location at start-up without using them, and contained the Google Play Billing library without selling anything.)

If you tap Share or Print on the Report tab, the report goes to the app or printer you choose (for example email, a messaging app, cloud storage, a print service or Save as PDF). That only happens when you do it.

4. Deleting your data

Clearing the app's storage in Android's settings, or uninstalling the app, deletes all of its data from your phone. You can also delete single tools, service records and the example data in the app. If Android backup is turned on for your phone, Android may keep a copy of the app's data in your own Google account backup, which you manage in your Google account. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on our side; you can still write to us with any question.

5. Children

The app is a work tool for adults and is not directed at children.

6. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
