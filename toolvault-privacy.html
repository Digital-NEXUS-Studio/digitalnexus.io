1. Introduction & Overview

This Privacy Policy governs the use of ToolVault: Jobsite Inventory, developed and distributed by Digital NEXUS. We are committed to transparency, user autonomy, and protecting your personal data in full compliance with Google Play Store Developer Program Policies and international privacy regulations (including GDPR and CCPA).

2. Data Collection, Transmission & Usage

ToolVault provides equipment tracking, barcode checkout, and maintenance logs for jobsite tools. All tool serial numbers, team assignments, and maintenance records are saved offline on your device.

3. Permissions & Device Access

Requires Camera permission to scan tool asset tags and barcodes.

4. Third-Party Services & Libraries

Operates fully offline without external tracking or analytics SDKs.

5. Data Retention, Security & Deletion

We implement robust technical encryption standards (HTTPS/TLS) for any data transmitted over networks. Offline data is stored securely in sandbox app storage on your device. You have the right to request deletion of your account and any associated support communications at any time by contacting our privacy compliance desk.

6. Children's Privacy

This application is intended for adult and professional users (age 18 and older) and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.
