JobProof: Field GPS & Quote: Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026
Developer: Digital NEXUS (digitalnexus.studio)
Package: com.jobproof.nexus

1. Summary

JobProof keeps your job photos, client details and quotes on your phone. Digital NEXUS does not collect, receive, sell or share any of your data. The app has no account, no ads and no analytics. There is nothing to buy.

2. What the app stores

Your projects: the client's name, phone, email and job address; job photos with the time, GPS coordinates, street address, compass heading and your notes and markings; and quote line items, prices and terms. This data is saved in the app's private storage on your device. The app never sends it to us; section 4 describes what does leave your phone.

3. Permissions

  • Camera: to take job photos inside the app.
  • Location: to stamp a photo with where it was taken, only while you use the app. The app does not track your location in the background.

The app asks for both when it starts; you can refuse and still use the rest of the app. You can also add an existing photo with Android's file picker; the app only gets the photo you pick. The app does not use the microphone or your contacts.

4. Internet use

To turn a photo's GPS coordinates into a street address, the app sends the latitude and longitude, and nothing else you entered, to OpenStreetMap's Nominatim service (nominatim.openstreetmap.org). That service is run by the OpenStreetMap Foundation under its own privacy policy and, like any web service, sees your device's IP address. Without a connection, the photo keeps its coordinates without a street address. When it opens, the app loads its fonts from Google Fonts. Like any web request, this lets Google see your device's IP address; it contains nothing you have entered. The app contains the Google Play Billing library, which talks to the Google Play app on your phone. The app takes no payments: nothing can be bought in it.

5. Printing

If you print a quote or report, Android's print screen opens. You choose a printer, or Save as PDF and where to save the file. That only happens when you do it, and the print service you choose is covered by its own privacy policy.

6. Deleting your data

You can delete photos and projects in the app. Clearing the app's storage in Android's settings, or uninstalling the app, deletes all of its data from your phone. If Android backup is turned on for your phone, Android may keep a copy of the app's data in your own Google account backup, which you manage in your Google account. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on our side; you can still write to us with any question.

7. Children

The app is a work tool for adults and is not directed at children.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
