1. Introduction & Overview

This Privacy Policy governs the use of JobProof: Field GPS Camera & Quick Estimates, developed and distributed by Digital NEXUS. We are committed to transparency, user autonomy, and protecting your personal data in full compliance with Google Play Store Developer Program Policies and international privacy regulations (including GDPR and CCPA).

2. Data Collection, Transmission & Usage

JobProof collects precise GPS coordinates only when you actively snap a jobsite photo to stamp geographic verification and timestamp watermarks onto contractor work logs. No background location tracking occurs. All estimate line items and client records are stored locally in your device's sandbox.

3. Permissions & Device Access

Requires Camera access to capture field proof photos and Fine Location access while the app is actively in use to generate verifiable GPS coordinates.

4. Third-Party Services & Libraries

PDF invoice generation runs locally. Google Play Billing handles subscription licensing.

5. Data Retention, Security & Deletion

We implement robust technical encryption standards (HTTPS/TLS) for any data transmitted over networks. Offline data is stored securely in sandbox app storage on your device. You have the right to request deletion of your account and any associated support communications at any time by contacting our privacy compliance desk.

6. Children's Privacy

This application is intended for adult and professional users (age 18 and older) and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.
