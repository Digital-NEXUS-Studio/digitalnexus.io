StormLog: Site Runoff Log: Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026
Developer: Digital NEXUS (digitalnexus.studio)
Package: com.stormlog.nexus

1. Summary

StormLog: Site Runoff Log stores everything you enter only on your phone. Digital NEXUS does not collect, receive, sell or share any of your data. The app has no account, no ads, no analytics and no in-app purchases.

2. What the app stores

The app keeps a record of construction-site stormwater inspections: site details, controls and their condition, rain readings and corrective actions. This data is saved in the app's private storage on your device. It is never sent to us or to anyone else by the app.

3. Permissions

The app asks for no device permissions: no camera, microphone, location, contacts or files. The current release declares zero internet or network-state permissions in its Android manifest, bundles its UI assets locally, and works 100% offline with zero network requests.

4. Sharing and printing

If you tap Share or Print, the report goes to the app or printer you choose (for example email, a messaging app, cloud storage or a print service). That only happens when you do it, and what that app does with the report is covered by its own privacy policy.

5. Deleting your data

You can delete any record in the app. Clearing the app's storage or uninstalling the app deletes all of its data from your phone. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on our side; you can still write to us with any question.

6. Children

The app is a work tool for adults and is not directed at children.

7. Independence

StormLog: Site Runoff Log is an independent app by Digital NEXUS. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to the U.S. Environmental Protection Agency (EPA), any state agency or any permitting authority.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
