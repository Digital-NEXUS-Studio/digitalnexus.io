1. What CrewTalk provides

CrewTalk lets field teams hold-to-talk over internet crew channels using invite codes (live WebRTC audio relayed via Cloudflare Workers and LiveKit Cloud) and record local voice-log notes on the device for replay.

  • Anyone who receives a channel invite code can join that channel; share channel codes only with people you trust.
  • The audible siren cue sounds locally on-device unless you are transmitting in a live room and does not contact emergency services or replace certified jobsite emergency communications.
  • Do not rely on CrewTalk as an emergency dispatch or life-safety alerting system.
