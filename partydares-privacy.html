Party Dares — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 11, 2026
Developer Brand: Digital NEXUS (digitalnexus.studio)
Package Identifier: com.partydares.nexus

1. Summary

Party Dares is a 100% offline pass-and-play social party game. Digital NEXUS does not collect, receive, transmit, sell, or share any personal data. The app has no user accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no in-app purchases.

2. What the app stores locally

Player names, game scores, shields, selected forfeits, and match settings are saved only inside private app local storage on your device so your hangout session stays intact if you switch apps. Nothing leaves your phone.

3. Permissions

Party Dares asks for zero runtime permissions (no camera, microphone, location, contacts, or file access). Party Dares declares zero network (INTERNET / ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE) permissions in its Android manifest and makes zero outbound network requests.

4. Internet & Offline Operation

All game modes, sound synthesis, animations, and challenge decks are bundled directly inside the app and run offline without external servers or font CDNs.

5. Payments

Party Dares includes no Google Play Billing library or subscriptions, and no money is involved anywhere in the game.

6. Sharing

If you tap Share on a final forfeit screen, only the plain-text verdict summary you choose to send is passed to Android's native share sheet.

7. Deleting Your Data

Resetting the match in the app, clearing app storage in Android Settings, or uninstalling Party Dares permanently removes all saved local player rosters and scores.
