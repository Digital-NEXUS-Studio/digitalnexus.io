FleetDVIR Compliance Policy

Privacy Policy for FleetDVIR: Commercial DOT Pre-Trip Inspection

Package Name: com.benbdira.fleetdvir

Effective Date: October 16, 2026

FleetDVIR is a commercial driver vehicle inspection report (DVIR) mobile application published by DigitalNexus (domain: digitalnexus.io). This policy describes how vehicle inspection records, defect photographs, and user information are handled.

1. Information Collected & How It Is Used

  • Vehicle Inspection Records: Truck and trailer inspection checklists, odometer readings, defect notes, and driver sign-offs are saved locally on the driver's device to ensure 100% offline functionality in remote transport corridors.
  • Camera & Defect Photos: The app requests camera access exclusively to allow drivers to photograph mechanical defects (e.g. brakes, tires, lights) for safety documentation. Photos remain under user control and can be exported as PDF reports.
  • Optional Location Tagging: Drivers may optionally record GPS coordinates on inspection reports to satisfy DOT/FMCSA proof-of-presence requirements. Background location tracking is never performed.
  • No Selling of Personal Data: DigitalNexus does not sell, rent, monetize, or transmit inspection data or contact details to third-party advertisers or data brokers.

2. Permissions Requested

  • android.permission.CAMERA: Required to photograph vehicle defects.
  • android.permission.ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION / ACCESS_COARSE_LOCATION: Used solely to stamp GPS coordinates onto the inspection log when enabled.
  • android.permission.INTERNET: Required for Google Play subscription verification and sending inspection reports via user-initiated email or cloud sharing.

3. In-App Subscriptions & Payments

All subscription processing is handled securely by Google Play Billing. We never collect or store payment card numbers, bank details, or billing addresses.

4. Data Retention and Deletion

Drivers and fleet managers can delete any individual inspection report, photo, or history record within the app settings. To request full deletion of any support correspondence or account records, email fredjbdira@gmail.com.
