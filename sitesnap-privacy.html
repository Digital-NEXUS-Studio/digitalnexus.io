SiteSnap: Daily Log: Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026
Developer: Digital NEXUS (digitalnexus.studio)
Package: com.sitesnap.nexus

1. Summary

SiteSnap keeps construction daily logs and site hazard notes on your phone. Digital NEXUS does not collect, receive, sell or share any of your data. The app has no account, no ads and no analytics.

2. What the app stores

Your projects (name, job code, address, client and superintendent), daily logs (date, shift, weather, temperatures, rain, delays, crews and hours, equipment, deliveries, notes and who signed the log) and hazard notes (title, severity, location, GPS coordinates, photo, corrective action, status and due date). This data is saved in the app's private storage on your device. The app never sends it to us or to anyone else.

3. Permissions

  • Location: from version 1.0.2, the app asks for location permission only when you tap Get GPS on a hazard note, never when it starts. The position is saved with that hazard on your phone and never sent anywhere. The app does not track your location. If the phone gives no position, the app records "Location unavailable"; it never stores made-up coordinates. (Version 1.0.1 asked for location at start-up and could save invented coordinates when GPS failed.)
  • Photos: you pick a photo with Android's file picker. The app has no camera permission and does not read your other photos.

The app also declares the standard internet and network-state permissions, which need no approval. It makes no network requests: everything works offline.

4. Payments

The app is free. It contains no billing library and nothing can be bought in it.

5. Deleting your data

You can delete hazard notes and crew and equipment entries in the app. Clearing the app's storage in Android's settings, or uninstalling the app, deletes all of its data from your phone. If Android backup is turned on for your phone, Android may keep a copy of the app's data in your own Google account backup, which you manage in your Google account. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on our side; you can still write to us with any question.

6. Children

The app is a work tool for adults and is not directed at children.

7. Independence

SiteSnap is an independent app by Digital NEXUS. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to the U.S. Occupational Safety and Health Administration (OSHA) or any government agency, and its logs are your own records, not official reports.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
