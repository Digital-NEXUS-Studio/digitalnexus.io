JobProof
Android · Contractors · Tradespeople

Jobsite photos with GPS and time, and a PDF quote before you leave

JobProof is a jobsite camera and quick-quote app for contractors and tradespeople. Take photos stamped with location and time, mark them up, and turn a site visit into a PDF quote.

Get it on Google Play See features

What it does

GPS and time-stamped photos

  • Each photo is stamped with GPS coordinates, the address when available, and the date and time.
  • Tag photos as before, in progress, after, rough-in, damage or compliance.

Mark up photos

  • Draw measurement lines and callouts on your photos.

Quick quotes

  • Add labor, materials and other line items; set your tax rate and deposit percentage.
  • Create a PDF quote with your company details and the site photos, then save or share it from Android's print screen.

Organized by job, works offline

  • Photos and the quote stay together for each job and client.
  • Jobs and photos are stored on your phone. No account needed.

Questions

How much does JobProof cost?

Every feature is free.

Does it upload my photos?

No. Jobs and photos are stored on your phone. You decide what to share.

Can I send the quote to my client?

Yes. Create the PDF and share it from Android's print screen by email, message or any app you use.
