Privacy Policy — FlowTest Pro: Backflow Tester

Application: FlowTest Pro: Backflow Tester (com.flowtest.nexus)
Developer & Publisher: Digital NEXUS
Effective Date: October 18, 2026

1. Overview & Scope

This Privacy Policy governs the use of FlowTest Pro: Backflow Tester (package identifier com.flowtest.nexus), a mobile application engineered for differential pressure gauge logging, RPZ/DCVA backflow assembly inspection, and municipal water purveyor certification, published by Digital NEXUS.

2. Offline-First Architecture & Local Data Storage

FlowTest Pro: Backflow Tester is designed with an offline-first architecture. All field inspection logs, calculations, compliance records, and user configurations are stored locally on your device using encrypted local storage (SQLite / IndexedDB / SharedPreferences). Digital NEXUS does not collect, sell, rent, or transmit your personal or commercial inspection records to external cloud servers.

3. Device Permissions

  • Microphone / Sensors (where applicable): Used strictly locally on-device for real-time acoustic or telemetry measurements and never recorded or transmitted externally.
  • Local Storage & File Export: Used solely to generate and save PDF/CSV inspection reports directly on your device when initiated by you.

4. Third-Party Services & Data Sharing

We do not sell or share personal data with third-party data brokers or advertising networks. Any optional premium license unlocks or subscriptions are processed securely by Google Play Billing.

5. Data Retention & Deletion

Because all application data resides locally on your device, you retain 100% control over your records. Clearing the application storage or uninstalling FlowTest Pro: Backflow Tester permanently deletes all locally stored data immediately.

6. Contact Us

For privacy inquiries, compliance questions, or support regarding FlowTest Pro: Backflow Tester (com.flowtest.nexus), please contact Digital NEXUS at support@digitalnexus.com.
