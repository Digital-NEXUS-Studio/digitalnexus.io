FlowTest: Backflow Test Log: Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 11, 2026
Developer: Digital NEXUS (digitalnexus.studio)
Package: com.flowtest.nexus

1. Summary

FlowTest keeps your backflow test records on your phone. Digital NEXUS does not collect, receive, sell or share any of your data. The app has no account, no ads and no analytics.

2. What the app stores

Your test records (customer, site address, location on site, assembly type, size, manufacturer, model and serial number, test date, gauge readings, notes and result) and your tester details (your name, the tester certification number you type in, your test kit serial and its calibration date). This data is saved in the app's private storage on your device. The app never sends it to us or to anyone else.

3. Permissions

The app asks for no device permissions: no camera, microphone, location, contacts or files. The current release declares zero internet or network-state permissions in its Android manifest.

4. Internet use

From version 1.0.1 the app makes no network requests: it works offline and does not send anything you enter over the internet. (Version 1.0.0 loaded its fonts from Google Fonts when it opened.)

5. Payments

The app is free. From version 1.0.1 it contains no billing library and nothing can be bought in it. (Version 1.0.0 showed paid plans that unlocked without any payment; they are gone.)

6. Sharing and printing

If you tap Share or Print on a record, it goes to the app or printer you choose (for example email, a messaging app, a print service or Save as PDF). That only happens when you do it, and what that app does with the record is covered by its own privacy policy.

7. Deleting your data

You can delete single records and the example records in the app. Clearing the app's storage in Android's settings, or uninstalling the app, deletes all of its data from your phone. If Android backup is turned on for your phone, Android may keep a copy of the app's data in your own Google account backup, which you manage in your Google account. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on our side; you can still write to us with any question.

8. Children

The app is a work tool for adults and is not directed at children.

9. Independence

FlowTest is an independent app by Digital NEXUS. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any water utility, government agency or certification body, and its records are your own, not official test reports.

10. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
