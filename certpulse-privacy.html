CertPulse: CE & Nurse Tracker: Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026
Developer: Digital NEXUS (digitalnexus.studio)
Package: com.certpulse.nexus

1. Summary

CertPulse keeps your licences, continuing-education (CE) courses and certifications on your phone. Digital NEXUS does not collect, receive, sell or share any of your data. The app has no account, no ads and no analytics. No payment can be made in the app.

2. What the app stores

Your profile (name, email and, if you add them, hospital or staffing agency), state licences (profession, state, licence number, dates and CE hours), CE courses (title, provider, date, hours and a certificate file name) and clinical certifications (type, issuing body, number and dates). This data is saved in the app's private storage on your device. The app never sends it to us or to anyone else. The app does not ask for patient information.

3. Permissions

The app asks for no device permissions: no camera, microphone, location, contacts, photos or files. It also declares the standard internet and network-state permissions, which need no approval. The app's features work offline, and it does not send anything you enter over the internet. The app contains the Google Play Billing library, which talks to the Google Play app on your phone. No payment can be made in the app.

4. Deleting your data

You can delete CE courses in the app. Clearing the app's storage in Android's settings, or uninstalling the app, deletes all of its data from your phone. If Android backup is turned on for your phone, Android may keep a copy of the app's data in your own Google account backup, which you manage in your Google account. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on our side; you can still write to us with any question.

5. Children

The app is a work tool for adults and is not directed at children.

6. Independence

CertPulse is an independent app by Digital NEXUS. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by or connected to any state board of nursing, the National Council of State Boards of Nursing (NCSBN), any certifying body or any employer. Check renewal requirements with your licensing board.

7. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
