CrewTalk: Voice Log Privacy Policy

Effective Date: November 6, 2026 (applies to app version 1.0.2 and later)
Developer: Digital NEXUS
Package Identifier: com.crewtalk.nexus

1. What CrewTalk does

CrewTalk: Voice Log records short voice notes with a hold-to-record button and keeps them in a log on your phone so you can play them back. It does not send voice or any other data to other phones, to us, or to anyone else.

2. Data the app handles, and where it stays

  • Voice recordings you make, with their time and length.
  • Channel labels and the display name you choose, plus your app settings.

All of this is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. CrewTalk has no account and no server, and we never receive or see your recordings or settings.

3. Permissions

  • Microphone: used only while you hold the record button.
  • Vibration: short vibration on the record button (can be turned off in Settings).
  • Network access: part of the Android app package; CrewTalk does not upload your recordings or any personal data.

4. Third parties

CrewTalk contains no advertising, analytics or tracking services and shares no data with third parties.

5. Retention and deletion

The app keeps your 30 most recent voice notes; older notes and their recordings are deleted automatically. You can delete everything at any time by clearing the app's storage in Android settings or by uninstalling the app. Because we never receive your data, we hold nothing about you that could be deleted on request.

Changes

A future version with live push-to-talk between phones will send voice over the internet. If that happens, this policy will be updated before that version is released, describing exactly what is sent, to whom, and how long it is kept.

6. Children's Privacy

This application is intended for adult and professional users (age 18 and older) and does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.
