LienShield: Mechanics Lien Log: Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 9, 2026
Developer: Digital NEXUS (digitalnexus.studio)
Package: com.lienshield.nexus

1. Summary

LienShield keeps your construction payment projects and notice dates on your phone. Digital NEXUS does not collect, receive, sell or share any of your data. The app has no account, no ads and no analytics. There is nothing to buy.

2. What the app stores

Your projects: project name, job address, state, your role, contract amount, amount paid and balance, first and last furnishing dates, property owner, general contractor, lender, notice and deadline dates, and certified-mail tracking numbers. This data is saved in the app's private storage on your device. The app never sends it to us or to anyone else.

3. Permissions

The app asks for no device permissions: no camera, microphone, location, contacts or files. It also declares the standard internet and network-state permissions, which need no approval.

4. Internet use

The app's features work offline, and it does not send anything you enter over the internet. When it opens, the app loads its fonts from Google Fonts. Like any web request, this lets Google see your device's IP address; it contains nothing you have entered. The app contains the Google Play Billing library, which talks to the Google Play app on your phone. The app takes no payments: nothing can be bought in it.

5. Printing and copying

If you tap Print, Android's print screen opens with your report. You choose a printer, or Save as PDF and where to save the file. That only happens when you do it, and the print service you choose is covered by its own privacy policy. If you tap Copy, the text goes to your phone's clipboard.

6. Deleting your data

Clearing the app's storage in Android's settings, or uninstalling the app, deletes all of its data from your phone. The current version has no button to delete a single project. If Android backup is turned on for your phone, Android may keep a copy of the app's data in your own Google account backup, which you manage in your Google account. Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to delete on our side; you can still write to us with any question.

7. Children

The app is a work tool for adults and is not directed at children.

8. Independence

LienShield is an independent app by Digital NEXUS. It is not legal advice, and it is not affiliated with any court, government agency or law firm. The deadlines it shows are estimates; check them against your state's law or with a lawyer.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date.
